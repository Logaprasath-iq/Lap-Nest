Laptop Screen & Display Panel Replacement

Restoring vibrant, true-to-life color visuals with original manufacturer display glass. We service non-touch, 10-point capacitive touch, 144Hz–240Hz gaming panels, and Apple Retina assemblies.

Turnaround: 45 to 90 Minutes 1-Year Zero Dead-Pixel Warranty

Common Failure Symptoms

  • Spiderweb impact cracks from dropped laptops
  • Vertical color lines or flickering backlight strips
  • "Black Screen" with faint image under torchlight
  • Bleeding ink stains spreading across the matrix
  • Hinge tear causing EDP cable severed connection

Our Technical Procedure

  • 01 Battery isolation to protect backlight rail fuse
  • 02 Ultrasonic bezel separation with thermal pad
  • 03 Exact panel model & 30/40-pin EDP verification
  • 04 Factory-grade pull-tab adhesive alignment
  • 05 True-tone RGB color calibration & pixel check
