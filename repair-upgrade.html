Cleanroom Bench Protocol

Common Hardware Symptoms & Engineering Solutions

Before authorizing an expensive motherboard replacement, consult our component-level repair reference matrix.

Symptom 01

Completely Dead (0.00A Current)

Root Cause: Shorted 19V/20V main power rail high-side MOSFET or decoupling ceramic capacitor.

Bench Fix: FLIR infrared thermal detection, micro-soldering replacement of shorted element. Turnaround: 2–4 hours.

Symptom 02

Power LED Active, No Screen Display

Root Cause: Corrupted SPI BIOS ROM chip, faulty Embedded Controller (EC/KBC), or missing VCCIO power rail.

Bench Fix: High-speed hardware SPI desolder & clean ME region reflashing. Turnaround: Same Day.

Symptom 03

Battery at 0% / "Plugged in, Not Charging"

Root Cause: Blown current sensing resistor, blown charging IC (ISL/BQ series), or broken SMBus communication clock.

Bench Fix: Logic board charging IC micro-soldering and battery gas gauge calibration. Turnaround: 3–5 hours.

Symptom 04

Freezes & Visual Artifacts in 3D Gaming

Root Cause: Micro-fractured solder joints under high-heat GPU VRAM chips from repeated thermal expansion.

Bench Fix: Automated optical BGA reballing with leaded solder spheres + PTM7950 phase-change repasting. Turnaround: 24 hours.

Symptom 05

Liquid Spill (Water, Coffee, Juice)

Root Cause: High-voltage backlight rail (30V–50V) shorting into low-voltage CPU data lines through liquid conductivity.

Bench Fix: 40kHz ultrasonic chemical wash, corroded micro-trace reconstruction with insulated enamel copper jumper wire.

Symptom 06

Constant Thermal Throttling / Fan Roar

Root Cause: Pumped-out dried silicone thermal paste, copper vapor chamber pressure loss, or blocked radiator fins.

Bench Fix: Vapor chamber thermal conductivity bench test, deep ultrasonic radiator clean, Honeywell PTM7950 application.
