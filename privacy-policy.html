Privacy Policy & Client Data Charter

Effective Date: January 01, 2026 • LAPNEST Inc. Chennai

1. Hard Drive & Storage Data Confidentiality

When submitting a laptop for repair or upgrade at our Sholinganallur facility, customer personal and corporate data remains strictly protected. Under our Cleanroom Data Charter, storage devices (NVMe, SATA SSDs, HDDs) are disconnected and stored in individual RFID-tagged anti-static Faraday enclosures during motherboard micro-soldering.

2. Information We Collect

We collect basic contact details (Name, Mobile Phone, Delivery Address, Email) exclusively for the execution of sales dispatches, repair status SMS updates, and GST tax invoice generation.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Marketing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with telemarketing agencies, ad networks, or data brokers. Information is shared strictly with authorized logistics couriers for device delivery across Tamil Nadu.

4. Data Deletion on Trade-In Laptops

All laptops sold to or traded in with LAPNEST undergo Department of Defense (DoD 5220.22-M) 3-pass cryptographic data wiping before any refurbished resale.
